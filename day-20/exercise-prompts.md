### Problem Statement

You are tasked with creating a 3-day travel itinerary for a budget-friendly trip to Kyoto, Japan.
Your final output should include:

  A day-wise plan (morning, afternoon, evening).
  Estimated costs in USD.
  Cultural/local experiences (not just touristy).
  Food suggestions for each day.
  A final summary with a total budget.

The challenge is that you cannot ask ChatGPT for everything in one single prompt.
Instead, you must design 5–10 prompts that progressively refine the solution.
